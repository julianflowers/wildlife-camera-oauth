## Wildlife Camera Archive — Privacy Policy
Wildlife Camera Archive is a personal application used to transfer wildlife camera recordings from a Raspberry Pi to the owner's Google Drive account using rclone.
The application accesses Google Drive only with the account owner's authorisation.
Wildlife recordings and associated metadata are stored in the owner's Google Drive account.
The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. It does not operate a public service or collect data from other users.
Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
